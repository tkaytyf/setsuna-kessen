What is Setsuna Kessen?

Setsuna Kessen (刹那決戦) is an anime fighting game on Roblox where every fight is decided in an instant.

What is a Kessen?

A Kessen is a short, sanctioned duel decided in a single decisive moment.

Is it on Roblox?

Are there codes?

SETSUNA, FIRSTBLOOD, and AURELBAY grant gems and cosmetics, redeemable at launch. Redeem all three to unlock your first skin.
