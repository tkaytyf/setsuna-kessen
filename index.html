The story

Seven seconds changed everything.

Seven years ago, the city of Aurel Bay stopped. Clocks, traffic, voices — frozen for seven seconds. When the world moved again, hundreds of people woke with a Resonance: a personal rule the world would briefly obey.

Vesperreach Academy

Vesperreach was founded to teach control, duty, and the art of the Kessen — a short, sanctioned duel decided in a single decisive moment. Graduates who pass the Registry's trials become Wardens, licensed to protect the city.

Your story begins late.

You arrive as a first-year whose Resonance woke late. Orientation Week starts now: a campus tour, a gym lesson, and your first Kessen under a proctor's eye.
But in the plaza, the memorial clocks have started losing time again...

How to play

Every fight is decided in an instant.

  1. 01

    Strike

    M1 Combo

    Chain four quick hits. The last hit cancels straight into an ability.

  2. 02

    Defend

    Block & Parry

    Block to soak damage, but it drains stamina. Parry at the last instant to stun your attacker and get a free dash back.

  3. 03

    Move

    Dash & Stamina

    Dash in any direction, plus one air dash. Dashing and blocking share one stamina bar — run dry and you're open.

  4. 04

    Unleash

    Awakening

    Deal damage and land parries to fill your meter. At 100%, enter Setsuna: about 8–10 seconds of a powered-up kit and your fighter's ultimate. One decisive moment can win the round.
